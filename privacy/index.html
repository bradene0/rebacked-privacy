Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Rebacked is a local-first ebook library application for macOS. Your library and its associated data are primarily stored locally on your Mac.

Information Rebacked Collects

Rebacked does not require you to create an account.

Rebacked does not collect or transmit your ebook library, notes, reading history, collections, ratings, custom metadata, or other locally stored library information to Rebacked.

Rebacked does not include third-party advertising or analytics.

Local Data

Information used to manage your library is stored locally on your Mac. This may include book metadata, collections, ratings, reading status, notes, custom fields, saved searches, cover information, and references to your source book files.

Rebacked does not operate a cloud service that uploads or stores your library.

Book and Folder Access

Rebacked accesses book files, folders, and export locations that you explicitly select or authorize through macOS.

Rebacked may retain permission to access locations you have previously authorized so that features such as reopening source books and exporting files continue to work after the application is restarted.

These permissions are managed using macOS security mechanisms and are used only to provide Rebacked's library and file-management functionality.

Original Book Files

Rebacked is designed to preserve your original source books.

When Rebacked creates an EPUB containing edited metadata or cover artwork, the modified book is exported as a separate file rather than silently replacing the original source book.

For formats where Rebacked does not support modifying the book itself, such as PDF, MOBI, or AZW3, Rebacked may export an unchanged copy of the original file.

Optional Online Features

Some Rebacked features can optionally communicate with third-party online services to retrieve book information or cover artwork.

When you choose to use one of these features, information necessary to perform the request may be sent directly to the applicable third-party service. Depending on the feature, this information may include a book title, author, ISBN, or similar book metadata.

Third-party services may also receive technical information normally associated with an internet request, such as your IP address.

Information received by a third-party service is governed by that service's own privacy policy and terms.

Open Library

Rebacked can use Open Library to search for book information and cover artwork.

When you use these features, book information needed for the search is sent to Open Library. Rebacked may temporarily cache provider responses to improve performance and reduce unnecessary requests.

Open Library is operated independently of Rebacked.

Goodreads

Rebacked can open Goodreads book pages in your default web browser.

When you choose to open Goodreads or another external website, your browser communicates directly with that service. Your use of the website is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

Kindle and Kobo

Rebacked may prepare or copy supported book files for use with Kindle or Kobo workflows.

Rebacked does not operate Amazon, Kindle, Kobo, or their associated services. Information you provide directly to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Analytics and Advertising

Rebacked does not include third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, advertising SDKs, or targeted advertising.

Data Sharing

Rebacked does not sell your personal information.

Rebacked does not operate a service that receives a copy of your ebook library or locally stored library data.

Information may be sent to third-party services only when necessary to perform optional online features that you choose to use.

Data Retention and Deletion

Rebacked's library data is stored locally on your Mac. You control that data through Rebacked and macOS.

Because Rebacked does not require a user account and does not maintain a cloud copy of your library, there is no Rebacked server-side library associated with your identity that needs to be separately deleted.

Removing Rebacked's local application data may remove locally stored library information. Your original ebook files remain separate from Rebacked's library database unless you choose to delete those files yourself.

Children's Privacy

Rebacked is not designed to collect personal information from children, and Rebacked does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Rebacked's functionality or privacy practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.

Contact

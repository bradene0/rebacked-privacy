Supported Formats

Rebacked supports EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and AZW3 library management.

Does Rebacked modify my original books?

Rebacked is designed to preserve your original source files. EPUB metadata and cover changes are written to a separate exported copy rather than silently replacing the source book.

Why can't Rebacked modify MOBI or AZW3 files?

Rebacked can manage those formats in your library, but exports the original source file unchanged.

What happens if I move a source book?

Rebacked can reconnect an existing library entry to a moved source file using Locate Source.

Kindle and Kobo

Rebacked can prepare supported EPUB and PDF files for manual Kindle and Kobo workflows. Rebacked does not operate Amazon, Kindle, Kobo, or their associated services.

DRM

Rebacked does not remove or circumvent DRM.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Rebacked is a local-first ebook library application for macOS. Your library and associated data are primarily stored locally on your Mac.

Information Rebacked Collects

Rebacked does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your ebook library, notes, reading history, collections, ratings, custom metadata, or other locally stored library information to Rebacked.

Rebacked does not include third-party advertising or analytics.

Local Data

Rebacked may locally store book metadata, collections, ratings, reading status, notes, custom fields, saved searches, cover information, and references to source book files.

Rebacked does not operate a cloud service that uploads or stores your ebook library.

Book and Folder Access

Rebacked accesses files, folders, and export locations that you explicitly select or authorize through macOS.

Rebacked may retain permission to locations you previously authorized so that source access and export workflows continue working after the application is restarted.

Original Book Files

Rebacked is designed to preserve original source books. Modified EPUB files are exported separately rather than silently replacing the source.

PDF, MOBI, and AZW3 exports may consist of unchanged copies of the original source files.

Optional Online Features

Some Rebacked features can optionally communicate with third-party online services to retrieve book information or cover artwork.

When you choose to use one of these features, information needed for the request, such as title, author, ISBN, or similar book metadata, may be sent directly to the applicable service.

Those services may also receive technical information normally associated with an internet request, such as your IP address.

Open Library

Rebacked can use Open Library to search for book information and cover artwork. Provider responses may be temporarily cached to improve performance and reduce unnecessary requests.

Goodreads

Rebacked can open Goodreads book pages in your default browser. Your interaction with Goodreads is governed by Goodreads' own privacy policy and terms.

Kindle and Kobo

Information you provide directly to Amazon, Kindle, Kobo, or related services is governed by those services' respective privacy policies and terms.

Data Sharing

Rebacked does not sell your personal information and does not operate a service that receives a copy of your ebook library.

Information may be sent to third-party services only when necessary for optional online features you choose to use.

Data Retention and Deletion

Rebacked library data is stored locally on your Mac. Because Rebacked does not require an account or maintain a cloud copy of your library, there is no server-side Rebacked library associated with your identity.

Children's Privacy

Rebacked does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Rebacked's functionality or privacy practices change. The revision date above indicates the most recent update.

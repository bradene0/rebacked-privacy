Rebacked Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Rebacked is a local-first ebook library application for macOS. Your library, book files, metadata, notes, collections, ratings, and other library information are stored locally on your Mac.

Information Rebacked Collects

Rebacked does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your ebook library, notes, reading history, collections, ratings, or other locally stored library information to Rebacked.

Rebacked does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Book Files

Rebacked accesses book files and folders that you explicitly select or authorize through macOS. These files are used locally to provide library management, metadata, cover, organization, and export features.

Rebacked is designed to preserve your original source books. When Rebacked creates a modified EPUB, the modified book is exported as a separate file rather than silently replacing the original.

Online Features

Some Rebacked features can optionally access third-party online services, such as book metadata or cover-art providers.

When you use one of these features, information necessary to perform the request, such as a book title, author, or ISBN, may be sent directly to the applicable third-party service.

Those services may receive technical information normally associated with an internet request, such as your IP address. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Goodreads

Rebacked can open Goodreads book pages in your web browser. When you choose to open an external website, your interaction with that website is governed by the website's own privacy policy and terms.

Kindle and Kobo

Rebacked may prepare or copy supported book files for use with Kindle or Kobo workflows. Rebacked does not operate Amazon, Kindle, Kobo, or their associated services. Any information you provide directly to those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing

Rebacked does not sell your personal information.

Rebacked does not operate a service that receives your ebook library or locally stored library data. Information is sent to third-party services only when necessary for optional online features that you choose to use.

Data Retention

Rebacked library data is stored locally on your Mac. You control that data through the application and macOS. Because Rebacked does not maintain a user account or cloud library service, Rebacked does not maintain a server-side copy of your library for you to request deletion from.

Children's Privacy

Rebacked does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Rebacked's functionality or privacy practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
